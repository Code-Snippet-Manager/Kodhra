PRODUCT DIRECTION
============================================================

Build KODHRA around three major pillars:

1. STORE
   Everything developers create.

2. SHARE
   Everything developers discover.

3. BUILD
   Everything developers need.

Important product decision:
KODHRA should not become a collection of random tools. Every feature should connect to a developer's projects, code, knowledge, or workflow.

Long-term goal:
"One Workspace. Everything a Developer Needs."
